Manage Sites

HIB works only on the sites listed here. On every other site it does nothing: it does not read, change or collect anything from the page. Firefox lists "all websites" for this add-on because a video often comes from another host than the page you added, so HIB must be allowed to reach it.

On the sites you add, HIB loads only what it needs: the picture or video you open, its subtitles, and for picture links the page that holds the picture. While you watch, the player keeps the video's segments in memory for smooth playback; nothing is saved to disk and it is cleared when you close the player. HIB sends nothing to its developer or to any server of its own.
