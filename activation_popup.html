How HIB works
  • Hover a card for the hover delay: the video is scanned and the card shows a − when it is ready.
  • Right-click a card with a − : the HIB video player opens. Any other card: the image popup.
  • Ctrl + Right Shift does the same as a right-click on the card under the pointer. Ctrl + right-click stays with Imagus Reborn.
  • In the player, ↑ / ↓ change the volume by the Volume step in the player's title bar.
