Beta features

For pages like Mega or Google Drive. Turn it on, reload the video page, start the video, then right-click the card as usual. Affects every page until turned off.
For videos that use the HEVC (H.265) codec. Firefox plays them with a decoder already on your computer. Turn this on and HIB checks for one; if none is found it points you to the Microsoft Store. HIB installs nothing.
